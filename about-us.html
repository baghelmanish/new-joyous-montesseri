About Us

Joyous Montessori: A Nurturing Environment for Your Child

Looking for a school that fosters your child’s natural curiosity and love for learning? Joyous Montessori in Keller, Texas, offers a unique early education experience based on the renowned Montessori philosophy.

Our dedicated, qualified and trained teachers provide a warm and supportive environment where children can explore, discover, and grow at their own pace. With a low student-to teacher ratio, we can give each child the individualized attention they deserve.

Why Choose Joyous Montessori?

  • Montessori Method: Our curriculum is designed to spark curiosity and foster independence.
  • Quality Care: Our experienced teachers are passionate about child development.
  • Safe and Secure Campus: Our school is in a safe and peaceful setting with ample outdoor play space.
  • Enrichment Programs: We offer a variety of activities to support your child’s social, emotional, and physical development.

Join the Joyous Montessori Family

We invite you to visit our school and see for yourself why Joyous Montessori is the perfect choice for your child. Let’s work together to create a bright future for the next generation.

🎯 Trust Our Methods

At Joyous Montessori, we believe in the Montessori philosophy, so we make sure that all our students get one on one time with our qualified teachers. We are very careful to keep classes small so that your children get the care and attention they need.

Some of our programs include:
  • Infant daycare
  • Pre-toddler class
  • Transitional toddler class
  • Primary class
  • Enrichment program
  • Summer program

⭐ Learn From the Best

All our teachers are qualified and trained and they are familiar with the Montessori philosophy. Our teachers ensure their classrooms are a safe and welcoming environment for the children, and they make sure they pay close, one on one attention to each child.

We pride ourselves on our low student to teacher ratios in our preschool because we want each child to get the care and attention he or she needs.

🌱 Let Your Child Excel

The Montessori system is designed to help your children excel not only in academics and the arts but also in every aspect of their lives. Instead of trying to force each child to fit the same learning mold, we develop learning programs that are crafted for each individual child.

Help your child reach his or her full potential by enrolling them in a joyous Montessori classroom. You will be amazed by how much your child can achieve in the right environment.
